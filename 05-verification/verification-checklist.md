# Verification Checklist

## My Personal Verification Checklist
Before I use or publish AI-assisted content, I check the following:

1. **Identify the content and purpose.** What is this output supposed to do, and who will use it?
2. **Compare against the original source.** Check names, dates, times, numbers, decisions, and requirements.
3. **Look for invented information.** Any detail not supported by the source must be removed or marked `[Not Specified]`.
4. **Check completeness.** Confirm that the output includes all important source details and has not omitted a critical item.
5. **Check wording and meaning.** Make sure summarization has not changed the original meaning or level of certainty.
6. **Check tone and format.** Confirm that the style is appropriate for the intended academic workplace audience.
7. **Check privacy and sensitivity.** Remove or anonymize any personal, confidential, or sensitive information.
8. **Document the decision.** Record what was checked, what was corrected, and whether the final version is approved by a human.
