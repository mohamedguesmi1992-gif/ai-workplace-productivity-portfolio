# Planning Workflow Example

## Goal
Plan and deliver a **60-minute student research seminar during the last week of September** for the university research team.

## Mechanisms
The plan will be delivered through five mechanisms:
1. Scope and format definition.
2. Venue and equipment confirmation.
3. Speaker and agenda coordination.
4. Participant communication.
5. Final readiness review.

## Phases and Tasks

| Phase | Mechanism | Actionable Tasks | Responsible Role | Completion Check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 — Define | Scope and format | Confirm seminar objective; confirm 60-minute duration; confirm 3 presentations + 10-minute Q&A | Research Assistant + Supervisor | Format documented and approved |
| Phase 2 — Secure Logistics | Venue and equipment | Confirm room; check seating and projector availability | Research Assistant | Room and equipment confirmed |
| Phase 3 — Build Program | Speaker and agenda coordination | Collect presentation titles; draft agenda; send agenda for supervisor review | Research Assistant / Sara / Supervisor | Final agenda approved |
| Phase 4 — Communicate | Participant communication | Draft participant email; verify date/time/location; send only after room is confirmed | Research Assistant | Participants receive accurate final details |
| Phase 5 — Final Review | Readiness check | Verify agenda, room, projector, presenter list, timing, and contact details | Research Assistant | All checklist items marked complete |

## Task Sequence
1. Confirm the seminar format and objective.
2. Confirm the room first because other communication depends on it.
3. Verify projector availability.
4. Collect presentation titles.
5. Prepare and review the agenda.
6. Draft and verify the participant email.
7. Send the participant email only after venue details are confirmed.
8. Conduct a final readiness check before the seminar.

## Human Decision Points
- A human supervisor approves the final seminar format and agenda.
- A human checks final room and equipment information.
- The research assistant reviews AI-assisted drafts before sending them.
- If details remain unknown, they stay marked as `[Not Specified]` rather than being invented.
