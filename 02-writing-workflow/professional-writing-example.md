# Professional Writing Example

**Scenario:** Notify a university research team that a meeting has been rescheduled.

## 1. AI-Generated Draft
**Subject:** Meeting Change

Hello everyone,

The meeting has been moved from Wednesday at 10:00 to Thursday at 11:00 because the supervisor has another commitment. Please confirm if the new time works for you.

Thanks.

**What happened in this step:** AI produced a short first draft from the structured prompt.

## 2. Fact Verification
I checked the draft against the source details:
- Original meeting time: **Wednesday at 10:00** — correct.
- New meeting time: **Thursday at 11:00** — correct.
- Reason: **supervisor has an academic committee commitment** — the draft said only “another commitment,” so it could be made more precise.
- Required response: **confirm availability** — correct.
- No extra names, dates, or facts were invented.

**What changed:** I identified one wording issue and confirmed that the factual details were accurate.

## 3. Tone Refinement
I improved the email by:
- changing the generic subject line to a clear professional subject;
- using a more courteous opening;
- stating the reason accurately;
- adding a polite request for early notice if someone has a conflict;
- keeping the message concise.

**What changed:** The tone became more professional and suitable for an academic workplace.

## 4. Human Review / Sign-off
I reviewed the final wording for accuracy, tone, clarity, and unnecessary information. I confirmed that the email contains only fictional/non-sensitive details and that the requested action is easy to understand.

**Human approval:** Approved for use in this fictional portfolio example.

## 5. Final Version
**Subject:** Research Team Meeting Rescheduled to Thursday at 11:00

Dear Team,

Please note that our research-team meeting originally scheduled for Wednesday at 10:00 has been rescheduled to **Thursday at 11:00** due to the supervisor's academic committee commitment.

The meeting agenda will remain unchanged. We will review current progress, discuss outstanding tasks, and confirm the next steps for the research activities.

Please reply to confirm whether you are available at the new time. If you have a scheduling conflict, let me know as soon as possible so that we can identify an appropriate solution.

Thank you for your flexibility and cooperation.

Kind regards,  
Academic Research Assistant
