## How I Will Use AI Safely
1. I will use AI as a support tool, not as the final decision-maker.
2. I will verify factual details against trusted source material.
3. I will avoid entering confidential, private, or sensitive data.
4. I will clearly mark unknown details as `[Not Specified]`.
5. I will review tone, bias, and appropriateness before sharing outputs.
6. I will keep human approval as the final step for important work.

## 30-Day Practical Adoption Goal
During the first month after training, I will use the workflows above on at least:
- 3 meeting-note summaries,
- 2 professional progress updates,
- 1 academic planning task.

At the end of the month, I will review whether the outputs were accurate, useful, and faster than my previous manual workflow. I will keep only the prompts and workflows that consistently improve quality without increasing risk.
